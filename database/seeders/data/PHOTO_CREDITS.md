# Coach Hire location hero photos — credits & licensing

Hero images for the /coach-hire/{city} pages, sourced from Wikimedia Commons and
converted to WebP + JPG (1600×700, plus 1024 and 640 width variants). Every image
is CC BY-SA or CC0 — free for commercial use.

The images are self-hosted in `public/images/hero/` and committed to the repo, so
they survive a fresh checkout without needing the `storage` symlink. Nothing is
hotlinked from Wikimedia.

**Attribution lives here, not on the page.** The on-hero credit caption was
removed, so this file (together with the `hero_image_credit` column, kept as a
record) is the attribution for the CC BY-SA photos below. CC BY-SA requires
crediting the author and naming the licence wherever the work is used — if you
publish these images without a visible credit, add one somewhere reachable from
the pages that use them (a footer or /credits page), or swap in photography you
own outright.
